## What drove the shift (from their own change notes + Aug assumptions email)

Documented in the Aug 6 assumptions email and the Sept “Changes to original quotation” lists:

1. **Pad depths roughly doubled in the pricing basis**  
   Aug assumption: pads **1.25 × 1.25 × 1.0 m**.  
   Sept assumption: pads **1.25 × 1.25 × 2.1 m**.  
   That alone is a major concrete / dig / spoil driver. Challenge with Integrum + SE before accepting.

2. **Foul drainage expanded hard**  
   Was: provisional **25 m** + 1 PPIC.  
   Now: **70 m** + **7** PPICs, based on draft GA-203 bathroom layout.  
   Ask for a measured take-off vs “draft GA allowance” — over-draining a draft plan is a classic QS uplift.

3. **Treatment plant moved from thin provisional to specified**  
   Was: **£4,000** provisional.  
   Now: finalised **6-person** unit + commissioning + power provisional (embedded in the bigger total).  
   Some of this is real scope completion; still check plant cost vs the old £4k allowance.

4. **Water harvesting tank added** — provisional **£6,000**  
   Tied to their note that no-dig / raised permeable road breaks gravity rainwater discharge into the road.  
   This may be a consequence of the driveway redesign, not an inevitable house cost. Challenge as a package with the road.

5. **Clay cart-away included** — **100 m³**  
   July explicitly **excluded** carting spoil. Alex had already flagged ~£3–4k as an acceptable clay-removal budget if spoil can stay on site. Confirm what rate they used for 100 m³ and how much can stay.

6. **Haul road accounting flipped**  
   July: ~£10.2k access road that finished as the permanent easy-grid road.  
   Sept: temporary haul road “can no longer form part of the permanent above-ground permeable road” and is **bundled into** the £154k foundations figure, while the permanent road becomes a **separate £27.4k no-dig** quote.  
   So road cost went from ~£10k all-in toward **temporary works inside foundations + £27k permanent** — a structural change in how access is priced, not a small tweak.

7. **Beam & block design firmed up**  
   July treated B&B as the economic route (with a £12k provisional mentioned in Aug email). Sept includes a provisional B&B design. Some uplift here may be legitimate firming; still ask for the B&B line vs the £12k provisional.

8. **Concrete slab option premium**  
   Sept slab package is **~£24.5k** above B&B. That is a client preference / SE route choice on top of the base uplift — not part of the July-to-Sept B&B shift.

## Path back toward £130k

Starting point for negotiation: **July £106.6k foundations + drainage** was their number on Option 2 B&B. Target £130k leaves ~**£23k** of legitimate firming room for real drainage growth, TP finalisation, and some spoil — without accepting the full £47k jump.

Priority challenges (highest £ first):

1. Pad depth basis **1.0 m → 2.1 m** — justify with SE/geology or price both.
2. Foul run **25 m / 1 MH → 70 m / 7 MH** — measured schedule vs draft GA.
3. Split **temporary haul** out of foundations and VE the **£27k no-dig** against July’s £10k easy-grid approach (or a hybrid).
4. Harvesting tank **£6k** — only if gravity into road truly impossible after road redesign.
5. Clay cart-away rate and on-site retention.
6. Stay on **B&B** unless SE/mortgage forces slab (slab alone costs more than the entire gap to £130k).

If they will not reopen July pricing, ask for a **reconciliation schedule**: July total → each change → September total, with £ against each line. Without that, the September figures are hard to trust as a fixed-price starting point.
