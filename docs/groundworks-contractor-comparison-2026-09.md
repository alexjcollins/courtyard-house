## Ranked recommendation

### Preferred commercial route

1. **Stay on beam & block** unless the SE (or lender) locks poured slab. The slab premium alone (~£24.5k at FGS; similar implied elsewhere) is larger than the entire gap from Sep B&B back to the £130k target.
2. **Primary negotiation target: FGS Pilcher Sep B&B**, using their own **July £106.6k** as the reconciliation baseline. Demand a line-by-line Jul→Sep bridge (pad depth, foul metres, TP, harvest tank, clay, haul accounting). Aim to land foundations+drainage at or under **£130k**.
3. **Use SE Groundworks Option 1 as competitive pressure and backup.** Their B&B+drainage core (£154.6k) matches FGS Sep — so FGS cannot claim uniqueness. Do **not** accept SE’s £206k headline or their payment/delay clauses as written. Strip patio PC, challenge spoil volumes at £42/m³, firm the Biodisc/SW PCs, await **Option 2**, then decide.
4. **Pinnacle:** Ask for (a) B&B alternative, (b) drainage add-on once drawings exist, (c) validity extension. Keep as slab benchmark / third pressure point — not current favourite.
5. **Distinctive:** Lowest priority. Ask whether they will price B&B + drainage with thinner prelims; otherwise park. £202k with blank drainage pages is not a serious contender against FGS/SE/Pinnacle.

### Risks to watch

| Risk | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| **Scope mismatch** | Pinnacle & Distinctive omit drainage; SE & FGS include it. Headline shopping without normalisation will pick the wrong winner. |
| **SE design lock-in** | Original SE poured slab vs cost-driven B&B pivot. Awarding a slab quote (Pinnacle/Distinctive/FGS Opt 2) re-locks the expensive route. |
| **Pad depth 2.1 m** | Shared cost driver (FGS Sep, Pinnacle assumption). Challenge with Integrum/SE geology before accepting. |
| **Estimate vs fixed** | SE is explicitly not fixed; PCs throughout. FGS Sep is more “quote-like” but still assumption-heavy. |
| **Road double-count** | FGS moved haul into foundations and re-priced permanent road at £27k. SE’s £29.7k driveway sits inside £206k. Don’t compare grand totals blindly. |
| **Incompleteness** | Gym, demo, incoming services, courtyard finish, steel — variously missing. Budget the gaps separately. |
| **Commercial terms** | SE late fees / warranty withholding; Pinnacle 50% materials deposit — negotiate before any letter of intent. |

### What to ask each contractor next

**FGS Pilcher**

1. Jul→Sep reconciliation schedule with £ against each change.
2. Price pads at SE-confirmed depth (1.0 m vs 2.1 m) as alternatives.
3. Measured foul schedule vs draft GA-203 allowance.
4. Split temporary haul out; VE no-dig vs July easy-grid / hybrid.
5. Confirm harvest tank necessity after road redesign.
6. Written confirmation B&B is acceptable to their assumptions / Monson fees path.

**SE Groundworks**

1. Send **Option 2** and state which foundation type it is.
2. Reissue Option 1 as a **fixed-price** offer with PCs converted or capped.
3. Strip or optionalise: patio £10k PC, ducts £6k, driveway (price separately).
4. Justify spoil volumes (reduce dig disposal 237 m³ vs reduce dig 132 m³ — check bulking/double count).
5. Rewrite payment/delay clauses; remove £150/day and warranty withholding.
6. Confirm gym 8×4 and demolition as separate numbered options.
7. Start-on-site realism without rushing incomplete design.

**Pinnacle**

1. Validity extension past 30 days.
2. Alternate price for **B&B** on same footing assumptions.
3. Drainage + TP provisional once Alex issues civil layout / ditch discharge scheme.
4. Site visit and depth confirmation vs 2.1 m assumption.
5. Gym 8×4 groundworks optional.

**Distinctive**

1. Confirm they can price **B&B** (not only Cellcore slab).
2. Drainage + external works once layouts available — or decline further rounds.
3. Justify / remove road sweeper and commercial-team prelims for a private rural build.
4. Clarify whether steelwork/carpentry (Alex’s wider ask) is coming separately or dropped.

## Bottom line (candid)

- **Best route:** **B&B**, not slab.
- **Best commercial play:** Negotiate **FGS** hard off their July number toward **≤ £130k** foundations+drainage; hold **SE** as a near-identical Sep competitor and programme backup once Option 2 and terms are cleaned up.
- **Do not award** on Pinnacle or Distinctive as they stand — incomplete and (Distinctive) expensive.
- **Nobody currently under £130k** except the superseded FGS July tender. The fight is to claw Sep pricing back, not to hunt a secret cheaper full-scope quote among these three.
